What we process, why and for how long

Visits. Each page view sends one event through our own server to PostHog: the page address, the referrer, your IP address and your browser's user agent, plus your time zone, language, screen and viewport size and the campaign tags (utm, ref) of the link you came from. PostHog uses the IP address and user agent only to compute a hash that changes every day, so that one visit can be told from another; it stores neither of them (it does keep the other fields listed here), sets no cookie and uses no browser storage. We also send the actions you take, such as a click on a call-to-action or the start of a quote request. Nothing is sent if your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is reached (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Server logs. The web server and the proxy in front of it log each request: your IP address, your browser's user agent, the time, the address requested and, if your browser sends it, the page you came from. The web server's log is capped at 30 MB and older lines are discarded as it fills; the proxy's log is deleted after 15 days. We use these logs to diagnose faults and security problems and to count visits per day and the kind of site they came from; the count is made on our server and keeps no IP address (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your email. If you write to support@revalx.eu, we keep your message and address to answer you and for the length of the business relationship that follows, then delete them (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)).

This site sets no cookie and shows no advertising.

Who receives data

PostHog Inc. (analytics, EU Cloud, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany). Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting, Helsinki, Finland). Forward Email (Forward Email LLC, United States) receives mail sent to support@revalx.eu, by its own policy does not store it, and passes it on to the operator's own mailbox at Google (Gmail, United States). Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States) sends our replies to mail sent to support@revalx.eu. It sends them from its EU region in Ireland and keeps a copy of each mail, its text included, in the United States.

Anthropic Ireland, Limited (Ireland, EU), which may transfer data to Anthropic, PBC (United States): the software tools we use to read the server logs and the mail sent to support@revalx.eu send what they read to Anthropic, to diagnose faults and security problems and to answer you. For these tools, Anthropic's role depends on the Claude account they run on. On our business (Team) accounts it is our processor under its data processing addendum. On a consumer plan (Claude Pro or Max) it is an independent controller under its own privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy), which also lets it use the data to prevent fraud and abuse, resolve security issues and meet its legal obligations. On the consumer plan we have switched off the setting that lets Anthropic use our conversations to train its models; its business terms already exclude that.

Transfers outside the EEA rely on standard contractual clauses (Forward Email, PostHog, Anthropic, Resend) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Google). We sell no personal data.
