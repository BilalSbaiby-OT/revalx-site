What we process, why and for how long

Visits. Each page view sends one event through our own server to PostHog: the page address, the referrer, your IP address and your browser's user agent. PostHog uses the IP address and user agent only to compute a hash that changes every day, so that one visit can be told from another; it stores neither of them, sets no cookie and uses no browser storage. Nothing is sent if your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is reached (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Server log. The web server writes your IP address, browser description, the time and the address requested to a rotating log capped at 30 MB; older lines are discarded as it fills. We read it only to diagnose faults and security problems (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your email. If you write to support@revalx.eu, we keep your message and address to answer you and for the length of the business relationship that follows, then delete them (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)).

This site sets no cookie and shows no advertising.

Who receives data

PostHog Inc. (analytics, EU Cloud, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany). Hetzner Online GmbH (hosting, Germany). Forward Email (Forward Email LLC, United States) receives mail sent to support@revalx.eu, by its own policy does not store it, and passes it on to the operator's own mailbox at Google (Gmail, United States).

Transfers outside the EEA rely on standard contractual clauses (Forward Email, PostHog) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Google). We sell no personal data.
