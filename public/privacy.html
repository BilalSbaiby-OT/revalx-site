Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party scripts. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, time) for security and to count visits; they are kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

If you email us or buy the audit, we use your shop URL and email address only to deliver the audit and answer you. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card details; we never see them. To access or delete your data, write to hello@revalx.eu.
