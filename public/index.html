FAQ

What is an AI visibility audit?

We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers would ask, record which shops and sources they cite, and compare that with your site. You get a list of what to change.

Do you guarantee I will be recommended?

No. Nobody controls what an assistant answers. The audit shows what is stopping your shop from being a clear answer today, and the fixes are the changes most likely to help.

What do I need to send?

Your shop URL, your main market, and optionally your top competitors. Nothing else. We never need access to your shop admin.

Which languages are covered?

The full audit covers two languages of your choice. The mini-audit uses your main market.

How fast is delivery?

Within 72 hours of your payment and the details we ask for.

Is there a subscription?

No. €149 is paid once.
